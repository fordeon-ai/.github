## What we do

Fordeon helps organizations turn business challenges into practical digital solutions. We run three independent offerings:

### DEX Services

Improve the way employees experience technology. Our DEX services center on **Baseera**, Fordeon's AI layer for digital employee experience in air-gapped environments. Baseera helps IT teams explore workplace data through conversational queries, tailored dashboards and summaries, without relying on cloud connectivity.

### Digital Marketing SaaS

Keep content planning and delivery moving. A subscription that combines a digital marketing platform with managed content planning, creation, review and coordination.

### Custom Solutions & AI Services

Build around the way your business works:

- Business applications
- Customer and employee portals
- System and API integrations
- AI assistants and knowledge tools
- Workflow automation
- Dashboards and operational views
